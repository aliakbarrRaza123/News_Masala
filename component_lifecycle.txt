<------------------------ Component LifeCycle  ----------------------->
1- Mount (birth of your Component)
2- Update (growth of your Component)
3- Unmount (death of your Component)


<------------------------ Methods in React Component LifeCycle  ----------------------->
1- ComponentDidMount()    {DOM render hone ke baad ye chalega}
2- ComponentDidUpdate()   {updating the DOM in response to new props,state changes or force update}
3- ComponentWillUnmount() {usually used to perform cleanups}
4- render()               {it runs during mounting and updating of your component}
4- render()               {it should be pure you can't change the state inside it}



<------------------------ Diagram for Component LifeCycle  ----------------------->
Mounting :-
1- constructor()
2- render()
3- ComponentDidMount()

Updating :-
1- render()
2- ComponentDidUpdate()

Unmounting :-
1- ComponentWillUnmount()
